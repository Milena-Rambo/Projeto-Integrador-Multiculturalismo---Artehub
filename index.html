Art without Borders

ArteHub is a school project created to value different cultures through art. Here you will find texts, images, videos and stories that show how differences can bring people closer together.

Meet the groupWhere we live

What is ArteHub?

ArteHub was born from a school assignment about multiculturalism. We are four students from different cities and communities in Paraná, and each of us has her own story, way of speaking and customs.

On this website, we bring together what we have learned and lived to show that art can be a bridge between people.

What is multiculturalism?

Multiculturalism is the coexistence of different cultures in the same society. It appears in languages, accents, religions, food, celebrations and in the traditions of each family and each place.

Recognizing this diversity helps fight prejudice and helps us see that no culture is better than another.

Art without Borders

“Art without Borders” is the proposal of our project. The idea is to use paintings, photographs, music, dance, literature and videos to present different cultural expressions.

We want to encourage coexistence based on respect, curiosity and appreciation of identities.

Art and cultural diversity

Art can express feelings, stories and knowledge in many ways. A song, a dance or a photograph can tell a lot about who we are and where we come from.

That is why we chose art as our path: it does not need to speak the same language to share a message.
