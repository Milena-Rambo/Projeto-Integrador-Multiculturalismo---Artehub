Where we live

Each member of the group lives in a different place in Paraná. Here we show a little of each one, with its communities, celebrations and traditions.

Milena and Fabiely — Alto Santa Fé and Nova Santa Rosa

This is the most special part of this page, because Alto Santa Fé is the place where Milena has always lived and where she lives today. Since Alto Santa Fé belongs to the municipality of Nova Santa Rosa, we will start with the municipality.

Nova Santa Rosa

LocationParaná, Brazil
Approximate population8 thousand inhabitants (information used in the project)

Nova Santa Rosa is a municipality in the countryside of Paraná. It is a place with a strong sense of community, rural characteristics and traditions that are passed down from generation to generation.

An important mark of the region is the influence of German immigration, which shows in the customs, the celebrations and the way the community organizes itself.

Alto Santa Fé

Alto Santa Fé is a small village, a district that belongs to the municipality of Nova Santa Rosa, in Paraná. It is not an independent municipality.

Milena lives in the countryside of Alto Santa Fé, where she has always lived. The estimate used in our project is about one thousand residents.

Please note: this number is only an estimate made by the group, not an official figure.

What the community is like

  • A small, rural community
  • Agriculture present in everyday life
  • German tradition
  • The Catholic Church
  • Clube Social União (the social club)
  • Community celebrations and dances
  • Oktoberfest
  • A German dance group
  • Dolls used in auctions
  • The characters Friz and Frida
  • The church festival, held in July
  • The history of the community itself

The church and the community

The community has a Catholic church, which turned 67 years old in the year mentioned in our original project.

In July, the church festival takes place, a moment when the community gets together.

School

The local school offers education up to the 9th grade. To attend high school, students have to look for other towns.

German culture in Alto Santa Fé

German tradition is one of the most striking features of the community. It appears mainly in the celebrations, the dances and the dance groups.

Oktoberfest is one of the best-known events, and the community also has a German dance group that keeps this tradition alive. Dances and community celebrations, such as those involving Clube Social União, are also part of this tradition.

Among the curiosities are the dolls used in auctions and the characters Friz and Frida, which are part of this world of festivals and traditions in the community.

For us, all of this shows how a culture that came from far away stays alive and blends with life in the countryside of Paraná.

UFO reports in the region

One of the curiosities that interests Milena the most is the reports of UFOs, or unidentified flying objects, in the Alto Santa Fé region.

These stories are part of the conversations and curiosities of the community. They spark interest and help show that every place has its own narratives.

Important: these are reports and curiosities told by the community. They are not scientifically proven events, and we do not mention names, dates or witnesses.

Fabiely — Nova Santa Rosa

Fabiely lives in Nova Santa Rosa, in Paraná, and knows the life of the region closely.

Her experience helps the group see how the culture of Nova Santa Rosa, marked by life in the countryside, German influence and community unity, shows up in everyday life.

Yasmin — Toledo

Yasmin lives in Toledo, a municipality in western Paraná. Toledo has urban characteristics and brings together people from different backgrounds, which is reflected in the variety of customs, food and events in the city.

In a larger city, diversity appears in the streets, in schools and in everyday encounters, and each person brings a little of their own story.

What we highlight

  • Culture
  • Gastronomy
  • Events
  • Traditions

Paolla — Maripá

Paolla lives in Maripá, a municipality in Paraná. It is a smaller town, where community life and the connection with the countryside are part of everyday life.

Like every place, Maripá has its local culture, its traditions and its celebrations, which help shape the identity of those who live there.

What we highlight

  • Characteristics of the town
  • Local culture
  • Traditions
  • Community
  • Celebrations

Cultural diversity between communities

Even though we live in different places, the four of us live in communities in the same state, and still each one has its own customs, celebrations and way of being.

When we share these experiences, we realize that differences do not push us apart. They teach us something new. An accent, a festival or a family tradition may seem like small details, but they tell the story of a people.

This is what ArteHub wants to show: people from different places can exchange experiences, learn from one another and value what is unique about each place.
